About neepora

neepora helps turn stiff AI-assisted drafts into clearer, more natural writing while preserving the writer's intended meaning. It does not invent research, evidence, citations or personal experience. Detector scores are useful signals, not proof of who wrote a text.

What neepora does

neepora revises sentence structure, flow, word choice and tone. It is designed for drafts you wrote or are allowed to use, including business writing, web content, reports, translations and work by non-native English speakers.

Our principles

1. Meaning comes before a detector score. The rewrite should still say what the original says.

2. No hidden tricks. We do not use invisible characters, homoglyphs, deliberate errors or formatting that breaks when copied.

3. No fabricated facts. We do not add research, evidence, citations, numbers or personal experiences that were not in the draft.

4. Limits stay visible. Detector scores can be wrong and may change when a detector changes.

5. Your text is processed to provide the service, not to train models. You can delete saved documents from your account.

Responsible use

neepora is for improving writing you have the right to use, in settings where AI assistance is allowed. What we do not allow, what detector scores mean and what stays your responsibility are set out on the Responsible use page.
