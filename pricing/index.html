Questions people ask

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Moving up to a bigger plan, or from monthly to yearly, starts right away: you pay only the difference for the rest of the period and get the extra words at once. Moving down to a smaller plan starts on your next billing date, with no charge today. Yearly to monthly can start when your year ends.

What happens to unused words?

On Basic, Pro and Max, words you do not use roll over for one month. Top-up words never expire. AI detector words never expire either: they stay until you use them.

What is your refund policy?

Payments are non-refundable, because the service starts as soon as you pay. One exception: if you email support@neepora.com within 24 hours of your first payment and have not used any words, we refund it minus a processing fee of 10% plus $1. Separately, if a completed rewrite scores below 90 on our AI detector, the credits spent on that document are returned automatically once per document.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from your account page in one click. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.

How are words counted?

By the words in the text you submit.

Do AI detector checks use my humanizer words?

No. AI detector words are a separate balance. Every account gets 1,000 free once. Each plan and top-up adds as many detector words as humanizer words, and detector words stay until you use them. There are no free refills.
