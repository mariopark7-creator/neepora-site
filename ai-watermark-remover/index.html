Do AI tools put watermarks in text?

There are two different things called an AI watermark, and only one of them can be removed by a cleaner like this.

Hidden characters. Invisible Unicode characters and unusual spaces end up in text copied from chat windows, PDFs, web pages and some writing apps. Whether a given AI tool adds them on purpose is rarely documented, but they are easy to find and easy to remove, and that is what this tool does.

Statistical watermarks. Researchers have shown that a model can be made to prefer certain words in a pattern only a matching detector can see (Kirchenbauer et al., 2023). That kind of watermark lives in the word choice itself. Deleting characters does not touch it; only rewriting the text does.

Three steps, a few seconds.

What the cleaner does not do

It does not change your words. Every sentence comes out exactly as it went in, minus the characters you cannot see.

It also does not make AI writing read as human. AI detectors look at how the writing flows: sentence length, word choice, how predictable each sentence is. Hidden characters play little or no part in that. To see how your text scores, use the free AI detector. To change how it reads while keeping what it says, use the AI humanizer.

Why hidden characters cause trouble

Documents and forms

Invisible characters break word counts, search, and character limits in application forms.

Code and data

A zero-width space in a variable name or a CSV value causes errors that are hard to spot.

Websites and CMS

Non-breaking spaces stop lines from wrapping and leave odd gaps on mobile.

Chat output

Markdown symbols turn into literal asterisks and hash marks in Word or Google Docs.

Questions people ask

Is the AI watermark remover free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and has no word limit.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The cleaning runs in your browser. The text never leaves your device.

Does it remove watermarks from AI text?

It removes the hidden characters and formatting leftovers that people usually mean by a watermark. A statistical watermark in the word choice cannot be removed by deleting characters; it needs a rewrite.

Will cleaned text pass an AI detector?

Not by itself. Detectors judge the writing, not invisible characters. Check the text with the AI detector, and rewrite it with the AI humanizer if it is flagged.

Does it change my wording?

No. Only invisible characters and the formatting options you select are changed. Every word stays the same.

Should I replace em dashes and curly quotes?

Only if you want to. They are normal punctuation, so those options are off by default. Turn them on if your document or form needs plain characters.
