Questions people ask

What is an AI humanizer?

An AI humanizer rewrites text from an AI tool so it reads the way a person writes, with natural sentence length, plain words and a human flow. Your meaning and facts stay the same.

Will it change what I wrote?

No. Facts, numbers, names, quotes and the order of your argument stay. neepora changes how the sentences are written, not what they say.

Is neepora free?

Yes, to start. Sign up free and get 1,000 words to use within 30 days. Paid plans add more words and longer texts.

How is it different from a paraphraser?

A paraphraser swaps words and keeps the same sentence shapes. neepora rewrites how each sentence is built, the way a person would, and keeps what it says.

Can I check the result?

Yes. Every result comes with a score from our own AI detector, with a sentence-by-sentence breakdown.

Is my text stored or used for training?

Your text is used only to produce your result. We do not use it to train models, and you can delete it at any time.
