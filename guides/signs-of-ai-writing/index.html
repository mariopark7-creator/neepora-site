Signs of AI writing: how to tell if something was written by AI

Seven things a careful reader can spot, the words that give AI text away, and the limits of judging by eye.

You can often tell that something was written by AI before you can say why. The reasons are specific, and once you have seen them they are hard to miss. Here is a paragraph of typical AI writing to work from:

"Artificial intelligence has fundamentally transformed the way organizations approach decision-making. By leveraging vast amounts of data, AI systems can identify patterns and generate insights that would be impossible for humans to detect on their own. Moreover, these technologies enable businesses to streamline operations, enhance customer experiences, and foster innovation across multiple sectors. Ultimately, organizations must strike a careful balance between harnessing the benefits of AI and addressing its potential risks."

1. Every sentence is about the same length

Count the words in each sentence above. None is short and none sprawls. They all land somewhere between a dozen and thirty words. People do not write like that for long. They get impatient, or they get carried away.

2. The same linking words

Moreover. Furthermore. Additionally. Ultimately. In conclusion. It is important to note that. AI text leans on these to move from one sentence to the next, usually at the very start of the sentence.

3. Lists of three

"Streamline operations, enhance customer experiences, and foster innovation." Three items, each a verb and an object, with a rhythm like a slogan. One of these is normal. One in every paragraph is a sign.

4. Paragraphs that open with a summary and close with a lesson

The first sentence announces the topic. The last one steps back and tells you what it all means, often with a word like "ultimately" or "balance". Human paragraphs sometimes just stop.

5. No particular detail

Which organizations? What data? What happened? The paragraph never says. AI writing is fluent about categories and thin on particulars: no names, no dates, no moment that only the writer would know.

6. Balanced to a fault

Benefits on one side, risks on the other, and a call for careful balance at the end. AI text rarely commits to a view, and it rarely sounds annoyed, amused or unsure.

7. Flawless and flat

No typos, no asides, no change of register. The tone on the last line is the tone on the first.

Common AI words and phrases

Some vocabulary shows up far more in AI text than in everyday writing: leverage, foster, streamline, enhance, navigate, landscape, realm, crucial, robust, seamless, delve, a testament to, plays a vital role. None of them is wrong. A paragraph with four of them is worth a second look.

What these signs cannot tell you

People write this way too. Anyone trained on the five-paragraph essay, anyone writing formally, and many people writing in a second language will show several of these habits. And a careful human edit removes most of them from an AI draft.

So the signs are a reason to read more closely. They are not proof. Read are AI detectors accurate? before you rely on any single signal.
