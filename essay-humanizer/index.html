Why AI essays get flagged

An AI-drafted essay has a recognizable shape. The sentences are all about the same length. Every paragraph opens by restating the point and closes with a neat lesson. The same linking words turn up again and again: furthermore, moreover, in conclusion.

A reader notices that as quickly as a detector does. Changing a few words does not help, because the shape underneath is still there.

What the essay humanizer keeps

Your argument

The thesis and the order of your points stay as you wrote them.

Quotes

Quoted passages are left word for word.

Citations

In-text citations and references stay attached to the right claims.

Facts

Names, dates and numbers are not changed, and nothing new is invented.

Essays, research papers and theses

A short response or a full dissertation chapter both work. The free plan takes 300 words per run, which is enough to try it on a paragraph. Paid plans take from 2,500 to 15,000 words per run, depending on the plan.

Longer papers are processed in sections and joined back together, so headings and structure survive. Changed sentences are underlined, which makes it quick to check the rewrite against your draft.

How to humanize an essay

A careful pass takes a few minutes.

  1. 1Check the draft with the AI detector to see which sentences read as AI.
  2. 2Paste the essay into the humanizer and press Humanize.
  3. 3Go through the underlined changes. Make sure each sentence still says what you meant.
  4. 4Check every quote and citation against your sources. That part is always your job.
  5. 5Read it aloud once. If a line does not sound like you, change it.

Use it within your school's rules

Schools differ on AI help. Some allow it for drafting or editing, some ask you to say when you used it, and some ban it for certain work.

The humanizer does not change which rule applies to you. Use it where AI assistance is allowed, and remember that the essay goes in under your name. Read our responsible use page.

Flagged for an essay you wrote yourself?

It happens. Formal writing gets flagged more often than casual writing, and so does English written as a second language. In one widely cited study, seven detectors flagged about 61% of essays written by non-native English students as AI.

If that is you, start with the highlighted sentences in the detector. They usually share one pattern, and rewriting those few lines yourself is often enough. More in are AI detectors accurate?

Does it keep my quotes and citations?

Yes. Quotes stay word for word, and citations, names, dates and numbers stay where you put them.

No. It rewrites a draft you already have. It does not add ideas, sources or content.

Nobody can promise that. Every result is scored by our own AI detector and the score is shown with the text, but different checkers often disagree about the same essay.
