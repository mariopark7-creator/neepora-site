What this AI rewriter changes, and what it leaves alone

It changes the writing. Sentences stop being the same length. The stock linking words go. Paragraphs no longer open with a summary and close with a lesson.

It leaves the content. Names, numbers, dates, quotes and citations stay exactly as they were, and so does the order of your argument. Nothing is invented and nothing you wrote is dropped.

Changed sentences are underlined in the result, so you can compare the rewrite with your draft line by line.

AI rewriter or paraphrasing tool?

A paraphrasing tool swaps words for synonyms and keeps the same sentence shapes. The text is different on the surface and identical underneath, which is why paraphrased AI text usually still reads as AI.

An AI text rewriter has to do more than that. It has to rebuild the sentence: change where it starts, how long it runs and what it leads into. That is the part a person does without thinking, and the part this tool is built for.

Before and after: one AI paragraph, rewritten

An example of the kind of change a rewrite makes. The facts stay put; the sentences around them are rebuilt. Your own result will be different, because every draft is.

AI draft

Remote work has fundamentally transformed the modern workplace. Furthermore, it offers numerous benefits, including increased flexibility and improved work-life balance. Additionally, companies can reduce overhead costs by up to 30%. In conclusion, remote work is a crucial trend that will continue to shape the future.

Rewritten

Remote work changed what an office is for. People get to set their own hours and see more of their families, and companies that let staff work from home can cut overhead by as much as 30%. That is not a passing trend. It will keep shaping how people work.

  • Kept: the 30% figure, the two benefits, the claim about the future.
  • Removed: the linking words furthermore, additionally and in conclusion.
  • Changed: sentence lengths now run from 6 to 29 words instead of four of similar length.

Rewriting AI text by hand

For a short piece you can do it yourself. These changes do most of the work.

  1. 1Vary sentence length on purpose. Put a four-word sentence next to a thirty-word one.
  2. 2Cut the stock linking words: furthermore, moreover, additionally, in conclusion.
  3. 3Replace a general claim with one concrete detail: a number, a name, a moment.
  4. 4Let some paragraphs just stop. Not every one needs a closing line.

Questions people ask

Is the AI rewriter free?

You get 1,000 words free when you sign up, to use within 30 days. Paid plans start at $16.58 a month on yearly billing.

Can it rewrite a single paragraph or sentence?

Yes. Paste as little or as much as you need. Longer text gives the rewriter more to work with, so a full paragraph usually comes out better than one sentence on its own.

How do I rewrite AI text in my own words?

Read the draft once for what it says, then write each point the way you would explain it to a friend: shorter sentences next to longer ones, fewer linking words, one concrete detail per claim. The AI rewriter does this sentence by sentence and keeps the facts in place, so you can edit from there.

Can I rewrite a whole paragraph at once?

Yes. The rewriter works best on full paragraphs, because it can change how sentences connect to each other, not only the words inside them.

Can it rewrite text I wrote myself?

Yes. It is built for AI drafts, but it accepts any English prose.

Does the rewrite change my meaning?

No. It changes how things are said, not what is said. Facts, numbers, names, quotes and citations are kept as they were.

Is this a paraphrasing tool?

Not quite. A paraphrasing tool mostly swaps words and keeps the sentence shapes. The AI rewriter rebuilds the sentences, which is what makes the text read differently.

Will the rewritten text pass AI detectors?

Every result is scored by our own AI detector and the score is shown with the text. No tool can guarantee a result on every detector.
