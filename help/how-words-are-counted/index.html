How words are counted

A run uses as many words as the text you submit. The length of the result does not change what you are charged.

Checking a text with the AI Detector has its own word balance: 1,000 free words per account, once, and each plan payment or top-up adds as many detector words as humanizer words. Detector words stay until you use them. Checking a humanized result is free.

If a completed rewrite scores below 90 on our detector, the words for that document are returned automatically, once per document. Every run is listed with its word count in your history.
